Tumpukan biji-bijian dan pasir

\(r=\frac{h}{2}\) (m)
\(V=\frac{\pi}{12}h^3\) (\(\mathrm{m}^{3}\))
\(\frac{dV}{dt}\) (\(\mathrm{m}^{3}/\mathrm{menit}\))
\(\frac{dh}{dt}\) (m/menit)

Tinggi dua kali jari-jari (tinggi = diameter): \(r=\frac{h}{2}\). Maka \(V=\frac13\pi r^2h=\frac{\pi}{12}h^3\) dan \(\frac{dV}{dt}=\frac{\pi}{4}h^2\frac{dh}{dt}\).
