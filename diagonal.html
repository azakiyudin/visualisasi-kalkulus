Diagonal persegi panjang

Sisi \(x\) bertambah \(\frac12\) cm/det dan sisi \(y\) berkurang \(\frac14\) cm/det. Saat \(x=3\) cm dan \(y=4\) cm, bagaimana diagonal \(l\) berubah?

Putar: laju dianggap tetap
\(l=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\) (cm)
\(\frac{dl}{dt}\) (cm/det)
diagonal

\(l^2=x^2+y^2\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{dl}{dt}=\frac{x\frac{dx}{dt}+y\frac{dy}{dt}}{l}=\frac{x/2-y/4}{l}\).
