Volume silinder

Tinggi \(h\) bertambah 1 cm/det, jari-jari \(r\) berkurang 1 cm/det. Saat \(h=6\) cm dan \(r=10\) cm, bagaimana volume berubah?

Putar: laju dianggap tetap
\(V=\pi r^2h\) (cm³)
\(\frac{dV}{dt}\) (cm³/det)
volume

\(V=\pi r^2h\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{dV}{dt}=\pi\left(2rh\frac{dr}{dt}+r^2\frac{dh}{dt}\right)=\pi\,(r^2-2rh)\) karena \(\frac{dr}{dt}=-1,\ \frac{dh}{dt}=1\).
